# Future-work ledger — merged from both final-scrutiny reviews

**Status: FACT SCAFFOLD.** One deduplicated table, ordered by value given these results. The two
independent Sep-3 reviews each shipped a future-work list (r1 §F, 15 items; r2 §F, 14 items);
they overlap heavily. Items that appear in both are marked **[both]**; single-source items carry
**[r1]** or **[r2]**. Hours and cost columns are the reviews' own estimates, kept as estimates —
nothing here has been run, and no number in this file is a result.

**The top three are the "next" set** — they are the three that change what the current results
mean, rather than adding surface:

1. **Identical-weights base-vs-base agent null** — the control this study does not have.
2. **Fresh-sample replication of the null artefacts** — turns a post-hoc reading into a measurement.
3. **Coverage-planning auditor** — the direct fix for the study's main finding.
